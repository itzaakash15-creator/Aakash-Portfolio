About Me

I’m Aakash K — a Marketer, Brand Builder, Creator, Speaker, and B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science based between Coimbatore and Tuticorin, Tamil Nadu.

I don’t fit into one box.

My work sits at the intersection of business, creativity, technology, and communication. I build brands, digital experiences, and ideas that move people — turning concepts into meaningful experiences through strategic marketing, high-retention content, and technology.

My journey started with content creation in 2020, experimenting with cameras, editing, storytelling, and audience psychology. Over time, that curiosity evolved into digital marketing, personal branding, commercial video production, client projects, and web development. Today, I combine that creative foundation with my growing knowledge of AI, data science, and engineering to build things that are both technically useful and creatively impactful.

I’ve worked on digital campaigns, personal brands, commercial video projects, websites, and IoT prototypes — from creating short-form content for businesses to developing technology-driven systems such as MineGuardian/MineCore, an IoT telemetry prototype designed for underground mine safety.
